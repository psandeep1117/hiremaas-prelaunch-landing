Fine Print, Stated Plainly

$20/mo is the Manager's fee — in addition to whatever you pay your AI/LLM provider, not a replacement for it. Model and token costs from whichever LLMs you connect are billed to you directly, at your own provider's rates.

Our orchestration layer is built to use tokens efficiently, but your total AI spend still depends on how much you actually run through it — that's your provider's bill, not ours, and we never mark it up.

Rate ladder: each cohort of 100 signups locks its rate for a full year. Cohort 01 (first 100): $20/mo. Cohort 02 (next 100): $25/mo. The rate keeps climbing by cohort after that — the earlier you sign up, the lower the rate you lock in.

We're also building a referral program — bring in a hire, you both get a month free — plus a paid track for anyone actively spreading the word. Details land once enrollment opens.
