Six personas. One manager.

Your manager doesn't rotate staff — it shifts posture. Each persona below ships standard, included in the flat fee.

P-01STRATEGY

The Intellectual

Strategic frameworks, deep research, and decisions built on evidence rather than instinct.

Also handles: picks the right model for the job — reasoning-heavy work, nuance, or privacy-sensitive tasks.
P-02CULTURE

The Artist

Creative direction, culture-building, and storytelling that actually moves people.

Also handles: reviews AI-generated creative for brand consistency and tone.
P-03COMMS

Rallies the team, celebrates wins, and casts a vision people want to follow.

Also handles: turns agent dashboards and reports into narratives people actually read.
P-04MEDIATION

Conflict resolution, political navigation, and bridge-building across silos.

P-06AI OPS

Also handles: human-in-the-loop checkpoints so agents never run further than you allow.
